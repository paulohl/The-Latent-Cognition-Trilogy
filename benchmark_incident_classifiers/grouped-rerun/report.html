Group-aware uncertainty and paired comparisons

Intervals use 2,000 paired bootstrap draws of whole held-out template components, stratified by each component's class. All components in this sample are label-pure; class group counts, not record counts, are held fixed. These intervals are conditional on the trained models and observed groups, not training-sample uncertainty. Small group counts, especially synthetic groups, make bootstrap intervals fragile; zero-width intervals do not prove certainty on unseen templates.

Accuracy differences are tested by swapping the two classifiers' correctness totals within whole template groups under a symmetry/exchangeability assumption: exact enumeration when ≤16 groups have nonzero accuracy differences, otherwise 10,000 random swaps with a plus-one correction. Zero-difference groups need not be enumerated. These are tests of accuracy, not macro-F1. Global Holm adjustment spans the 60 new grouped comparisons (three datasets × four settings × five alternatives versus word SVM), separately from the historical random-split tests. Macro-F1 intervals are pointwise and not multiplicity-adjusted. Conditional bootstrap precision does not establish that inferred components are truly independent campaigns.

Differences on synthetic incidents test transfer across these inferred lexical groups, not across new incident categories, campaigns or generator grammars. Likewise, a high score on the public corpora does not resolve mixed spam/phishing label semantics or source bias.

Runtime and reproducibility

Grouped fitting includes vectorization, and inference is median warmed time over three batch predictions, including vectorization, divided by test count. This run uses single-threaded numerical libraries while an unrelated DistilBERT job remains active. Treat wall-clock times as contended CPU measurements, not controlled speed ratios against yesterday's run or end-to-end incident-response latency.

Extract grouped_code_and_results.zip retaining the incident-benchmark directory, then run OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 incident-benchmark/grouped-rerun/run_grouped.py.
