The publisher describes the corpus as representative of a personal inbox, hand classified, with possible mistakes. Later mailboxes are not originally anonymized. We extracted, screened, subsetted, text-decoded, partially redacted and defanged selected records. These modifications are not endorsed by the original author.

## Enron curated non-spam emails

Source: Arifa Islam Champa, *Phishing Email Curated Datasets* (2023), Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8339691.
- Dataset landing page: https://zenodo.org/records/8339691
- Machine-readable record: https://zenodo.org/api/records/8339691
- Exact file: https://zenodo.org/api/records/8339691/files/Enron.csv/content
- Declared curator license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

We used only curator-labeled non-spam (`label=0`) records from a bounded file prefix; parsed, subsetted, partially redacted and defanged text. This is a curated derivative of historical Enron email, not a newly consented contemporary email collection. The curator's license declaration does not independently establish clearance of every original sender's rights. Names and signatures may remain despite selected identifier redaction. Check third-party rights and privacy before further redistribution or sensitive use.

The curator requests the following citations (reference details transcribed from dataset metadata):

1. A. I. Champa, M. F. Rabbi, and M. F. Zibran (2024). Why phishing emails escape detection: A closer look at the failure points. *12th International Symposium on Digital Forensics and Security (ISDFS)*, 1–6. IEEE.
2. A. I. Champa, Md F. Rabbi, and M. F. Zibran (2024). Curated datasets and feature analysis for phishing email detection with machine learning. *3rd IEEE International Conference on Computing and Machine Intelligence (ICMI)*, 1–7. IEEE.

## Added annotations and research artifacts

New provenance fields, grouping proxies, schema, scripts, synthetic baseline comparisons and documentation were added for this research task. Original source labels are retained, not relabeled as expert-verified ground truth. No endorsement is implied. All public records are transformed text only; attachments and original transport headers are excluded. See README.md for limitations, transformations and task restrictions.
