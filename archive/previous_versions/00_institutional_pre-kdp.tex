\section{Independent Scientific and Technical Research}
\label{Independent Scientific and Technical Research}

Zinnia Labs Publishing is an independent publishing initiative established to disseminate original scientific research, theoretical investigations, and technical contributions developed within the Zinnia Labs research program.

The imprint provides an institutional channel for research that has matured sufficiently to warrant public examination, critical discussion, and further scientific development, independently of its eventual publication through established scholarly venues.

\textbf{The Latent Cognition Trilogy} constitutes the inaugural publication of Zinnia Labs Publishing, establishing the foundation for a continuing series of research publications.

% \hypertarget{Editorial Principles}{}
\section{Editorial Principles}
\label{Editorial Principles} 

Zinnia Labs Publishing is committed to scientific transparency, intellectual attribution, research traceability, and the preservation of scholarly records.

Its publishing practices will be developed around documented procedures for manuscript preparation, editorial examination, version control, bibliographic identification, corrections, and research archiving.

Independent dissemination does not constitute independent peer review. Research findings, theoretical propositions, and experimental claims retain their respective evidentiary status regardless of publication format.

The imprint seeks to support the continuity of scientific inquiry by making original research accessible for examination, criticism, validation, and subsequent development.
