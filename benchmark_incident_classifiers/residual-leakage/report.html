Campaign and source boundaries remain unverified

The frozen records contain text, class, dataset name and generated IDs/groups—not verified campaign/thread IDs or dependable timestamps. Lexical components cannot determine whether two paraphrased messages share an actor or campaign. Consequently, neither absence of an edge nor passing .90 separation establishes campaign novelty.

The two public sources still contain 36 ≥.90 cross-source candidate pairs, including 13 byte-identical text pairs. Current baselines are trained/tested separately within each source, so these pairs are not direct within-model leakage in the present evaluation. They make the datasets overlapping evidence and could contaminate a future cross-source generalization experiment:

What can safely be claimed?

  • Supported: train/test disjointness under the documented ≥.90 detector and frozen manifests.
  • Not supported: complete template decontamination, verified campaign separation, phishing-only ground truth or independent public-source replication.
  • Broader similarities need review: inspect residual .85 pairs and shared prefixes before deciding which are unwanted duplicates versus legitimate contrastive/boilerplate cases. Do not move the threshold solely to obtain a preferred model ranking.
  • Recommended next evaluation: predefine full grouped cross-validation covering every component once, include threshold sensitivity, and obtain actual campaign/time provenance for an external holdout. Freeze any revised grouping rule before rerunning methods.

Reproducibility and limits

45 threshold rows, 15 frozen partitions and the current data/split hashes were checked. All exact input intersections and qualifying ≥.90 cross-boundary edge counts are asserted zero. Lower-score similarities, paraphrases, subjects/headers outside the 2,000-character cap, multilingual similarity, attachment content and campaign identity are not newly assessed. Shared-prefix matches do not prove score inflation; no new corrected leaderboard or statistical test is claimed.
